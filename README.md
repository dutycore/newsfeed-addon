News Feed brings the latest Minecraft and Laby Client news straight into your game, without alt-tabbing.

Four sources: Minecraft.net articles, Laby Client blog posts, new Laby Client cosmetics and r/Minecraft. Turn each one on or off.
Compact HUD panel: filter by source, scroll through the list and see at a glance what is New, Seen or Read. Mark everything as read with one click.
Read before you open: click a news item to read a short summary in-game, then open it in your browser or jump to the next one.
Save for later: bookmark interesting posts and read them when you have time. Saved items stay even after they leave the feed.
Pop-up alerts: a small notification with a soft sound when something new arrives. Choose which sources may notify you, and place the pop-up anywhere on your screen.
Stays out of your way: do-not-disturb during combat, keyword filters, adjustable opacity, and a keybind to show or hide the panel (starts minimized).
Works everywhere: every Minecraft version Laby Client supports (1.8.9 to 26.3), in English, Danish and German.
